Larkspur Lane Bakery: Staff Handbook (a made-up business for this demo)

Opening hours
The shop is open Tuesday to Sunday, 7am to 3pm. We are closed on Mondays. On public holidays we open 8am to 1pm, except Thanksgiving and Christmas Day, when we are closed.

Shifts
Bakers start at 4:30am. Counter staff start at 6:30am so the case is full before the doors open at 7. The schedule for the next two weeks is posted every Thursday by 5pm in the back office and in the staff group chat.

Swapping a shift
You can swap a shift with another trained team member. Both of you post the swap in the staff group chat and the manager on duty confirms it. A swap is not final until it is confirmed.

Calling out sick
Call or text the manager on duty at least two hours before your shift starts. A text to a co-worker does not count. If you are sick for more than two shifts in a row, let us know when you expect to be back.

Breaks
For a shift of six hours or more you get a 30-minute paid break. Shorter shifts get a 10-minute break. Breaks are taken in the back room, not at the counter, and no more than one counter person is on break at a time.

Time off
Ask for time off at least three weeks ahead using the form in the back office. Requests for the two weeks before Thanksgiving and the two weeks before Christmas are only approved in special cases, because those are our busiest weeks.

Staff discount and food
Staff get one free drink and one pastry per shift. Off the clock, staff get 30 percent off anything in the shop.

Uniform
Black shirt, the bakery apron (we provide two), closed-toe non-slip shoes. Long hair tied back. Bakers wear a hair net in the kitchen.

Pay
Pay is every other Friday by direct deposit. Tips from the counter jar and card tips are pooled and split by hours worked, paid with your regular pay.
