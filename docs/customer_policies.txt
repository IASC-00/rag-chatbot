Larkspur Lane Bakery: Customer Policies (a made-up business for this demo)

Refunds and exchanges
If something is not right, bring it back the same day with your receipt and we will replace it or refund it, your choice. After the same day we can offer a replacement but not a refund. Custom cakes cannot be refunded once they have been picked up, but tell us if anything was wrong and we will make it right.

Custom cake orders
Custom cakes need at least 72 hours notice. Wedding cakes and cakes for more than 50 people need at least three weeks. A 50 percent deposit is due when you order and the rest is due at pickup. Orders can be changed up to 48 hours before pickup.

Cancelling an order
Cancel at least 48 hours before pickup and we refund the deposit in full. Inside 48 hours the deposit is kept, because the ingredients have already been bought and the work has usually started.

Allergies
Everything is made in one kitchen that handles wheat, eggs, milk, tree nuts, peanuts and soy. We label which items contain these, but we cannot promise any item is free of traces. We do make a gluten-free loaf and gluten-free brownies, baked first thing in the morning on cleaned equipment, but they are not certified gluten-free.

Wholesale and catering
Cafes and offices can order wholesale with 24 hours notice, minimum order of two dozen items. Catering trays (pastry, sandwich or mixed) need 48 hours notice and can be delivered within 5 miles for a flat delivery charge.

Loyalty card
Every tenth drink is free. Stamps do not expire. The card is not needed if you give us your phone number at the counter.

Payment
We take cards, phone payments and cash. We do not take checks.
