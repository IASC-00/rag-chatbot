Larkspur Lane Bakery: Opening and Closing Checklist (a made-up business for this demo)

Opening (first baker, from 4:30am)
1. Turn off the alarm with your own code. Never share codes.
2. Turn on both deck ovens to 400 degrees and the convection oven to 350. They take about 40 minutes to heat.
3. Check the walk-in fridge and the two reach-in fridges. Write each temperature on the log sheet. All must read 41 degrees or below. If any is above 41, move the dairy to the other fridge and text the manager.
4. Pull the laminated dough from the walk-in for croissants. Proof boxes go on at 5am.
5. Bread goes in first, pastry second, gluten-free items are baked first of all on cleaned trays.

Opening (counter, from 6:30am)
1. Turn on the espresso machine and run a blank shot through each group head.
2. Count the till float. It should be 150 dollars. Write the count on the till sheet.
3. Fill the display case and write the allergen labels for anything new.
4. Unlock the front door at 7am, not before.

Closing (from 2:30pm)
1. Stop taking custom cake orders at the counter at 2:30pm; take a phone number and pass it to the manager.
2. At 3pm lock the front door and flip the sign.
3. Day-old bread goes into the donation crate, which the food bank collects Tuesdays and Fridays at 4pm.
4. Count the till, take out everything above the 150 dollar float, and put the rest in the safe with the till sheet.
5. Clean the espresso machine: backflush with cleaner, soak the portafilters, empty the drip tray.
6. Write the fridge temperatures on the log sheet again.
7. Last person out sets the alarm and checks the back door is locked.

If something breaks
Text the manager on duty with a photo. Do not try to repair the ovens or the espresso machine yourself. The repair company's number is on the inside of the office door.
